# Current collections and recoverable price evidence

Accepted on 24 October 2026. The bargain threshold defaults to 10%. Confirmed
below-market finds still require at least five independent sellers (or the
higher configured minimum), compatible configurations and observations within
24 hours. Sparse three/four-seller references remain indicative and have a
separate browsing view. Partial component baselines have a separate "Below
component value" label and filter; they cannot establish a whole-PC market
discount or resale opportunity.

The main public market uses the latest successful full category collection,
with searches filtering it offline. Refresh membership is published only after
collection, interpretation and comparison finish successfully. Failures and
cancellations preserve previous membership. Retained local observations outside
that collection remain accessible as dated history; bookmarks remain private
and independent of membership. Focused private searches keep their own snapshots.

Deterministic quoted seller facts fill omitted AI fields; disagreements remain
reviewable. An otherwise unambiguous one-model, whole-item GPU advertisement
may establish a single-card offer without a literal quantity. Explicit bundles,
multiple cards, ambiguous prices and conflicting source claims remain blocked.
Promotional gift values do not establish the item's asking price.

Individual AI failures fall back to local interpretation and allow subsequent
listings to proceed. Three consecutive failures stop further AI attempts in that
job; successful assessments reset that counter. Budget exhaustion stops AI
immediately. Existing collection limits, provider reservations and scheduled
allowances are unchanged. Clear evidence blockers distinguish uncollected,
sparse, outdated and ambiguous comparisons instead of one generic label.
Unavailable advertisements retain their observations while skipping detail, AI
and comparison admission, preserving bounded work for usable candidates.
